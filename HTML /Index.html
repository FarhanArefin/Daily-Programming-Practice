Rise Beyond

Rise Beyond is a short indie film or dramatic video production featuring the character Ethan Carter, played by actor James Holloway. The story serves as a highly motivational narrative centered around resilience, overcoming self-doubt, and moving past personal fears to reach one's full potential. Guided by his mentor, Professor Adams, and his childhood friend, Lena Mitchell, Ethan navigates steep financial struggles and systemic setbacks before ultimately achieving triumph.
